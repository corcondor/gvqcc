## Mathematics
For fixed unconditional reversible endpoint repair without new HMR, constant-inclusive function spaces L and T, a CCX changes one generator by one product. Thus both dimension deficits relative to their intersection require at least max(r-,r+) paid gates. This is not a general lower bound allowing adaptive new measurement. Rank-two simultaneous frame symmetry is (u*f)+(v*h)=u*(f+h)+(u+v)*h over F2. Temporal phase pair g*x*y + g*(x+h+k)*y = g*(h+k)*y; for two changing coordinates the mixed term h*k remains. Verified local cocycle uses exact entry h=q33: after omitting clear h, measured bit is h+k instead of k; add phase g*y*h at entry and j*l*h and m*h at exit, with late CX h on y. Keeps all original branch records. Lean proves the scalar and actual projected identities; BDD proves reachability, not full Lean circuit semantics.

## Literature use, not blanket implementation claims
- Jeon, Huang, Cai (2026), Measurement-Based Uncomputation from an Error Correction Perspective, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31605v1 , §IV.3.1–2: minimal syndrome and redundant phase coordinates. Applied deterministic temporal observation dictionary and alternate correction representation; no postselection.
- Find & Boyar, Multiplicative Complexity of Vector Valued Boolean Functions, https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.6169 : simultaneous output/AND sharing, abstract and relevant formulation consulted; our bounded quotient graph is not their coding-theoretic lower bound.
- Quadratic Boolean forms, https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(92)90235-8 : scalar quadratic-form rank result consulted; not assumed to imply a tight multivariate reversible repair cost.
- Recovering or Testing Extended-Affine Equivalence, https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2022.3166692 : quadratic Jacobian symmetry as related direction; not a port of EA solver.
- Boyar/Peralta, https://www.nist.gov/publications/new-combinational-logic-minimization-technique-applications-cryptology : separate nonlinear and linear minimization. We optimize native affine repair/phase after paid-gate search, not their full algorithm.
- Morse, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30238 : unrestricted nonlinear feedback and Boolean idempotence; abstract consulted, no transfer of its lower bound to this circuit.
