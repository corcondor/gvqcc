1. Width as variable: prioritize official cost, not width alone; 23Q abstract bound and PR119 22Q dense-rotation native witness separate.
2. Dual control synthesis: for weighted rows w and Boolean frame V, use a=V^T w, M=V^T diag(w)V. AND, XOR, complemented products score M_ij, a_i+a_j-2M_ij, a_i-M_ij. Use exact full-domain margin after LP; dual scores are heuristics, not a proof of physical savings.
