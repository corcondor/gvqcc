# 36Q research roadmap — AI/agent handoff

## Objective / 絶対条件
Find a *whole native* invsqrt circuit of maximum 36 physical slots (including all scratch operands and HMR events), output restoration, correct phase/channel on all legal inputs, and lower official cost than pinned competitive 37Q baseline. Under PR114's **fixed rotation/cost model only**, E_paid <= 1452.0539 is a useful screen; it is NOT the score criterion when rotations or synthesis precision change. Distinguish exact PR92 phase semantics from tolerance-based analytic target. No score claim without official verification and complete cost accounting.

## Four integrated theory workstreams / 4理論の概要
1. **Dynamic nonlinear quotient / 動的非線形商空間.** At cut t let D_t be legal reachable states with history h, and A_t=F2[x]/I(D_t) its Boolean function algebra. Represent physical lines by <=36 functions; a dropped logical value must either be reconstructible on D_t or be observationally irrelevant to the remaining channel. Do not require an arbitrary chart to preserve every obsolete coordinate. Carry weighted phase covector and classical records alongside the chart; affine rank (with constant included) is not physical width. Goal: direct chart updates instead of decode/recompute. PR102, PR107, PR114, PR115 donors.
2. **Higher-order relation transport / 高次関係輸送.** For a'=a+da,b'=b+db in Boolean algebra, a'b'+ab=a db+b da+da db. Carry factored expressions across multiple updates instead of clearing their products. Transport quadratic/cubic kernels through a changing chart using the exact legal domain. Search new intermediate factors not in old cache span. Dirty-toggle commutator: d<-d+F, t<-t+dG, d<-d+F,t<-t+dG gives t<-t+FG with d restored when F,G independent of d,t. For several commuting target updates G_i, the same shared F can be toggled once per pass. Nested factorization needs explicit interference checks; do not extrapolate rank savings to physical CCX. PR112/115/113.
3. **Nonlinear discrete summation by parts / 非線形離散部分積分.** Over F2, m_1 f_1+m_0 f_0=(m_1+m_0)f_1+m_0(f_1+f_0). Carry phase debt as a function of live data and independent classical record bits; use pullback through later gates, cancel kernels, discharge before destroying necessary controls or exposing output. Over integer turns use weighted modular sum, not F2 addition. Private HMR may be quotiented only after channel independence proven; independent records cannot be equated. PR92, PR103, PR109, PR113, PR114.
4. **Width-constrained joint tensor contraction / 幅制約付きテンソル共同縮約.** Multi-output product rank, exact support and shared factor lifetime are joint optimization variables. Finite GF2 rank-one atoms (l_i*r_i) feed multiple downstream roots, with physical allocate/borrow/release schedule. Sibling flag transport p*b + p*(1+b)=p allows one child flag to turn into the other with a CX; do not separately uncompute and recompute. Persistent parity gauge across a whole pivot group means a flag-XOR is inserted once, transported through all parity changes and removed once. V51 demonstrates both at every level of prefix trie. General multi-stage phase-lifted tensor synthesis is open.
