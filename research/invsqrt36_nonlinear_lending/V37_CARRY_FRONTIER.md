## Distinct search space
Joint vectorial quadratic transvection: unchanged coordinates U, changed outputs D, represent net XOR deltas by polynomials in U. For each nonzero output covector find a degree<=2 expression, factor alternating form into rank pairs; greedy binary matroid basis minimizes sum of computed per-covector costs over this finite candidate dictionary. Conjugate updates by output-only invertible linear map. 2064 pure-data windows,1271 eligible,62087 output masks,5093 quadratic fits,9 full basis fits, no paid-saving proposal. This restricted class does not cover arbitrary varying controls or HMR. Masked-output carry directly fits full c' after updating a'=a+c and HMR(c), 158 motifs, no quadratic fit.
Sample rank check (4096 input/history pairs): 3240 of 3343 fully occupied cuts have affine rank37 excluding constant; 103 rank36. The former are certified lower ranks on actual sample rows, not a quantum width lower bound.
