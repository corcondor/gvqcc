## A genuine obstruction to fixing degree gaps by changing a quadratic constant

Let L=H^2+s^2 X be the degree-32 squarefree locator of an affine F2-subspace, deg H=16 and s!=0. Let r^2=k and D=X(X^2+X+k)L. In characteristic 2:

A=XH+sX(X+r)
B=(X+r)H+sX
D=A^2+XB^2.
First Euclidean division: A+B=R=rH+sX(X+r+1), degree 16.
Second Euclidean division: B+(1+X/r)R=(s/r)X(X^2+X+k), degree 3.
