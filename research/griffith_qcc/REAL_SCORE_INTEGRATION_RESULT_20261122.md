## Why the 38 -> 37 STE search cannot work unchanged

For the five expensive native post-HMR restart banks **0,1,5,7,11**, the exact real KMB is 38 paid CCX in the selected interval. Treat its first product P0 as a separately computed **independent** 129th input, alongside 128 live wires. The later 37 ANDs define a vector quadratic map whose GF(2) polar tensor first-axis flattening rank is **74 in all five banks**, and whose output nonlinear effect rank is 30.

For a quadratic function implemented by k products of affine parities, polar flattening rank cannot exceed 2k. Thus later gates must satisfy **k >= 74/2 = 37**. The existing later stage already saturates this bound. With P0 fixed as a separate independent source, asking it to become 36 ANDs is mathematically impossible.

**Scope is restricted**: P0 is actually itself the product of two real inputs. This is *not* a global lower bound on 38 -> 37 if one changes P0 and the other nonlinear factors simultaneously, exploits legal reachable states, changes the intermediate representation, or redesigns HMR timing. Do not claim global optimality.
