## Method (own construction; no public loader file is used)
1. Frame change. The public head loaders split the address as low x = (a0,a1,a2), high y = (a3..a10): 153 high
   values and only 5 unreachable cells per output. With x = (a4,a5,a6) there are 160 high values and 61
   unreachable cells per output. The official verifier simulates only addresses 0..1218 (`np.arange(num_vectors)`
   in scoreinfra/circuits.py), so those cells are exact don't-cares.
2. Partial columns become linear relations among the high functions (12 for the heads, 6 for loader 0). The x = 0
   part of each relation is 30 quadratic equations solved by a small C++ search (`hsolve.cpp`). The five group tags
   are pure high functions in this frame: free controls for loaders 1, 5, 7, 11.
3. Cover: dual matroid-parity planner. The target has dimension 143 (not 151) and 2-dimensional low subspaces have
   non-zero kernels, so covers with **95** word products exist (annihilator dimension 20; design search `d20.cpp`,
   extraction `x44_extract95.py`). 94 or fewer were not found.
4. Cached functions: 138 per head, 149 for loader 0. Sliding window: phase 1 builds what the first 26-29 products
   need, one AND at a time inside a prescribed space (bit-sliced AVX2 search `phase1bs5.cpp`, per-level time limits
   and backtracking), then one AND per missing function (linear solve), measurement uncomputation with CZ-only phase
   fixes over live functions, and two closable "anchor" combinations so that the final uncomputation closes.
5. Loader 0 (its words cannot be used: the verifier also runs it backwards with batch 0 loaded, and it has no free
   tags). With the five tags kept live the final uncomputation never closes. Instead the tags are ordinary members
   of the needed space and the first five phase-1 products (built from address bits, hence closable) are kept to the
   end; the closure then succeeds on the first try (`make0q.py`).

(`w14u-p233a..d`, `w14u-l0q`, `w14u-probe`) ran with internet disabled (`kjob.py`, pushed with a copy of kv.py that
sets `enable_internet` false); `p233a`, `p233b` and `l0q` lost their circuits to a missing module and were redone.

No GPU.

- heads at 233: `sh run234.sh 12 29 600 1 5 7 11` (needs `cover95_k<k>_s1.pkl`; one attempt is 5-10 minutes with
  3 threads and succeeded 12 times in 34; it retries with new seeds). At 234: `sh run234.sh 11 29 600 ...`
  (more reliable, about 5 minutes per loader).

  (rewrites `family\`).

## Negative results (scope as tested)
- Frame change on the wired loaders: bit 0 of a wired delta is f(a4..a10) + g(a0..a3); with high low-bits its mixed
  rank is 6-7 per slot (banks 2, 3, 8, 12, 14), so it stops being free. No gain.
- Public frame x = (a0,a1,a2): its unreachable cells give only one relation; 144 cached ANDs is its minimum.
- 94- and 93-product covers: not found by the design search (best 71 of 94 and 50 of 93 independent). 95-product
  covers with unit-kernel designs give pools of only about 4 products; plane-kernel designs work.
- Planted rank-2 elements (21 for loader 1): 96-product covers with 11 planted pairs exist but do not reduce the
  rank of the early products' rows where the window is tight.
- Delaying the fourth low product until the window has saturated (`build3.py`, covers rich in a low hyperplane W6)
  makes the searches about five times cheaper, but the anchors cannot be placed: a W6-rich 95-cover has at most
  95 - 30 = 65 W6 products, and the anchors would have to use almost only non-W6 products (no anchor set fits).
- 232 per head (13 relations): by the search estimate an attempt would succeed about 2% of the time; not tried.
- 243 for loader 0: needs a W6-rich cover with about 60 W6 products (the current design has at most 55); not built.
